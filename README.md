# RAG Playground · Break the Meaning City

An interactive companion to the video **"RAG in 4 Levels: From Age 5 to Pro"** by Sanjay Mishra.

Ask a question, watch a librarian pull cards from a city of meanings, then break the system on purpose to see why real RAG apps fail. Every position and score on the page comes from a real retrieval run, not a mock-up.
